## Purpose

Complement the existing documentation with a visual overview of SuperPlane's architecture and design system.

I hope this gives newcomers, including readers with less technical experience, a clearer starting point. It can also give experienced contributors a shared reference for planning, maintenance, and review.

Focused views of individual workflows could follow over time.

Proposed implementation: [PR #8880](https://github.com/superplanehq/superplane/pull/8880).

## Examples

These images are initial examples. I would be happy to adjust the layout, colors, symbols, or level of detail to fit the project's preferences.
