## Purpose

I hope these maps can offer a more approachable starting point for new SuperPlane users and contributors, including readers with less technical experience.

They complement the existing guides with an overview of how the main parts fit together. Readers can start with the diagrams and follow documentation and source links at their own pace.

The same reference can help experienced contributors plan changes, discuss dependencies, maintain the system, and review contributions. Focused views of individual workflows could build on this overview in future contributions, where the community finds them useful.
